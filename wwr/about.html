History

Founded in 2010 by a small group of passionate river guides, Whitewater Rafting Adventures grew from a single raft and a dream into one of the region's premier outdoor outfitters. Over the last decade and a half, we have safely guided over 50,000 guests down some of the most challenging and scenic class III-V rapids. We remain family-owned and committed to environmental preservation.
